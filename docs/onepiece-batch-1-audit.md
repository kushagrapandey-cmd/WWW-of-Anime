## Form, calibration and limits

### 1. Monkey D. Luffy

Manga review targets: 1044–1049; 1070–1072; 1106–1111. Selected state: Gear Five with supported advanced Haki. Exclude unlimited reality rewriting and external food refills; Bajrang Gun needs windup. Shared comparisons: Above restored Nagato 744, below Hashirama 840.

### 2. Roronoa Zoro

Manga review targets: 1010; 1032–1035; 1110. Selected state: King of Hell three-sword style, Enma retained. Asura is an earlier retained technique, not a second permanent form or extra body. Shared comparisons: Above Fourth Raikage 604, below Killer B 699.

### 3. Nami

Manga review targets: 875–876; 1016. Selected state: Post-Wano Zeus housed in Clima-Tact. Zeus is the supported weapon partner, not Big Mom’s entire homie arsenal; no instant Emperor scaling. Shared comparisons: Above local Iruka band, below Kakuzu 481.

### 4. Usopp

Manga review targets: 646; 758; 1004; 1016. Selected state: Post-timeskip Black Kabuto and Pop Greens. Observation Haki awakening is not consistent future sight; no invented advanced Haki. Shared comparisons: Above local Iruka band, below Kakuzu 481.

### 5. Sanji

Manga review targets: 1031–1034; 1107. Selected state: Awakened exoskeleton with Ifrit Jambe. No Raid Suit invisibility; speed concealment is contextual, not permanent invisibility. Shared comparisons: Above Fourth Raikage 604, below Killer B 699.

### 6. Tony Tony Chopper

Manga review targets: 605; 1014–1017. Selected state: Wano extended Monster Point and retained forms. Thirty-minute limit and recovery included; medicine is contextual support, not instant universal healing. Shared comparisons: Above local Iruka band, below Kakuzu 481.

### 7. Nico Robin

Manga review targets: 1020–1021. Selected state: Onigashima Demonio Fleur, retained learned techniques. No confirmed Armament Haki based on black shading; spawned limbs share damage. Shared comparisons: Above local Iruka band, below Kakuzu 481.

### 8. Franky

Manga review targets: 636; 1019; 1104. Selected state: General Franky with supported cyborg arsenal. Mecha is equipment, not a teammate; General Cannon and Radical Beam operate in different equipment positions. Shared comparisons: Around Kakuzu 481, below Onoki 572.

### 9. Brook

Manga review targets: 643; 849–851. Selected state: Post-timeskip soul-enhanced swordsmanship. Revival already occurred once; no endless resurrection or automatic soul destruction. Shared comparisons: Above local Iruka band, below Kakuzu 481.

### 10. Jinbe

Manga review targets: 546; 629; 980; 1017–1018. Selected state: Wano/Egghead veteran karate with Armament Haki. No automatic internal-water knockout; unlimited ocean supply not assumed in neutral arenas. Shared comparisons: Above Onoki 572, below Fourth Raikage 604.

### 11. Portgas D. Ace

Manga review targets: 440–441; 572–574. Selected state: Supported living Flame-Flame Fruit combat state. No novel-derived techniques, invented awakening or inferred advanced Haki; use Banaro feats with retained techniques. Shared comparisons: Above Kakuzu 481, below Onoki 572.

### 12. Sabo

Manga review targets: 743–744; 751–752; 1085. Selected state: Post-Dressrosa Flame-Flame Fruit plus Dragon Claw. No assumed awakening or advanced Conqueror’s Haki; surviving escape is not defeating every pursuer. Shared comparisons: Above Fourth Raikage 604, below Killer B 699.

### 13. Shanks

Manga review targets: 434; 1055; 1079. Selected state: Manga-supported adult Haki swordsman. Exclude Film Red techniques, teleportation and invented named arsenal; Haki Burst is a descriptive ability label. Shared comparisons: Above restored Nagato 744, below Hashirama 840.

### 14. Edward Newgate

Manga review targets: 563–564; 576–577; 963–966. Selected state: Roger-era adult with supported quake and Haki skills. Prime flashback is directly shown, not invented; named attacks here are descriptive. Do not combine terminal Marineford injuries with healthy-prime endurance. Shared comparisons: Above restored Nagato 744, below Hashirama 840; within provisional 651–850 band.

### 15. Marshall D. Teach

Manga review targets: 440–441; 577; 1059; 1063–1064; 1081. Selected state: Post-timeskip dual-fruit Emperor. No assumed third fruit, awakening or Conqueror’s Haki; contact suppression is not universal cross-series nullification. Shared comparisons: Above restored Nagato 744, below Hashirama 840.

## Interpretation and remaining review

Conqueror’s Haki and Logia effects do not grant automatic crossover victories. Darkness suppresses demonstrated Devil Fruit powers through contact; it is not assumed to erase chakra or reiatsu. Soul effects do not automatically destroy every opponent. Equipment and partners already present in the supported form count as resources, not extra draft slots.

Zoro’s 651 and Sanji’s 663 illustrate a limitation of one scalar: Zoro has higher attack here, while Sanji’s speed, bodily resilience and sustained fighting raise the weighted total. This is not a claim that Sanji wins their duel. Teach’s 747 sits close to the Legendary boundary; small justified stat changes could alter rarity, so review the stats rather than treating a cutoff as a canon fact.

Whitebeard’s 794 uses demonstrated Roger-era adulthood with supported quake techniques retained from his established arsenal. Healthy-prime endurance is a cautious inference from limited flashback fights, not an unseen limitless form; confidence is low. Shanks is also low confidence because a few elite demonstrations cannot establish every dimension of prolonged combat.

Image queries use the roster’s canonical display names. Phase 3 must resolve spelling/order aliases such as Jinbe/Jinbei/Jimbei, Edward Newgate/Whitebeard and Marshall D. Teach/Blackbeard against the One Piece series, with low-confidence matches flagged.

Batch distribution: 4 Common, 4 Rare, 4 Epic, 3 Legendary, 0 Mythic. Do not rebalance stats to fill rarity quotas. All 120 records are needed for the eventual distribution and cross-series review.
