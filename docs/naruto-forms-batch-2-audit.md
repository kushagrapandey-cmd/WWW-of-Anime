## Scope and coherence

- Temari: early fan, retrieval/Kamatari and war toolkit remain separate; no unshown elemental upgrade or free summoned army.
- Kankuro: Crow, three-puppet loadout and war/Sasori equipment have different finite mechanisms. Puppet capture requires positioning; Sasori puppet is not an extra drafted teammate.
- Jiraiya: living base excludes sage boosts; imperfect Sage explicitly includes elder-toad cooperation and gathering time. Goemon is cooperative, not an invented independent elemental arsenal.
- Tsunade: early base retains blood-phobia limitations, first Creation Rebirth has stored-chakra/cell costs, and war Hundred Healings is finite. No undemonstrated young-prime boost.
- Orochimaru: functioning arms, sealed arms, deteriorating bridge vessel, true white snake, Eight Branches and restored White Zetsu host are distinct. Body-transfer rituals are resisted/conditional; Kabuto assistance and reanimated Hokage armies are excluded. No invented Sage Mode.
- Hashirama: living base/Sage and two reanimation eras are separate. Early restricted Edo does not inherit restored war power. War Sage/Gates does not automatically inherit the living thousand-hands feat. Sealing can stop Edo recovery.
- Tobirama: restricted invasion Edo and restored war Edo have different output. The living final-stand entry is a low-confidence retained-repertoire estimate, not a fully demonstrated living duel; water evidence comes from the earlier Edo depiction and teleportation from war recollection. Review before draft eligibility sign-off.
- Hiruzen: elderly living state includes fatal death-seal costs; war reanimation has observed sustained elements. Unseen young-prime power is not fabricated.
- Minato: living, reanimated base, brief reanimated Sage burst and Kurama Link are separate. Sage proficiency is poor in sustained combat; it does not stack with a removed Kurama. Marking, lost arms and beast transfer constrain abilities.
- Itachi: living non-Susanoo and terminal Susanoo have illness/eye costs; Edo restores endurance but remains sealable. Izanami needs a loop and eye sacrifice; Totsuka is contact-dependent, not an automatic win.
- Kisame: carried Samehada, swordless final fight and sword fusion differ. Samehada must cooperate; Great Shark cannot absorb Guy’s physical compressed-air attack. Full fusion retains personal techniques, not external refills.
- Deidara: ordinary clay, C4, Edo and C0 loadouts differ. Clay is finite; inhalation and lightning counters constrain C4. C0 kills the living user; no repeatable Edo suicide loop is inferred.
- Sasori: Hiruko, Kazekage puppet, puppet-body collection and reduced Edo body differ. Edo does not restore lost Iron Sand/puppet mechanisms. Poison needs applicable delivery and core vulnerability remains.
- Hidan: scythe base has no linked damage until blood/circle conditions exist. Immortality does not regenerate lost limbs or prevent immobilization.
- Kakuzu: hardened body, living five-heart masks and reduced war reanimation differ. Finite hearts, lightning counters and sealing prevent unrestricted endurance.
