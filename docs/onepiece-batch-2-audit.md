## Remaining audit items

Doflamingo’s 566 exceeds the original provisional city-anchor band (301–500). His utility, tactics and finite endurance influence the eight-stat composite; this is not a claim of measured island destruction. Keep the formula result and review the provisional anchor instead of clamping the score.

King’s defense and speed summarize a fighter who switches states. They do not mean his maximum protection and maximum speed coexist. Dedicated variants must preserve that tradeoff in the form backfill. Kaido and Big Mom similarly need their distinct supported body/attack states represented separately; their current records are selected peak toolkits, not completed variant catalogs.

Kuma’s chronology, conscious cyborg state and self-sacrifice make peak selection uncertain. Crocodile’s recent status does not prove an unseen power boost. Mihawk’s title and sparse serious fights leave numerical ceiling uncertain. These three are low confidence; no inferred awakening, hypothetical prime or automatic equality is added.

Batch distribution: 0 Common, 8 Rare, 5 Epic, 2 Legendary, 0 Mythic. Rarity is derived from score, not popularity or intended quota. Move translations vary; generic black-blade, repulsion and extraction labels describe supported actions rather than invented official attack titles.
