## Evidence and calibration limits

Official retrospectives and primary chapter locators were checked. Chapter ranges below are review targets, not a claim of reading every paid panel. Numerical ratings remain editorial game estimates. Full cross-series calibration is pending until One Piece and Bleach records exist.

Peak states must stay coherent: stabilized Ten-Tails Obito does not receive his incompatible Kamui intangibility; final Madara does not borrow Kakashi’s eye on top of his final two-Rinnegan state; Kaguya’s realm control is not a measured planetary-destruction calculation. Conditional attacks, prebuilt traps and outside armies retain their limits. No additional engine mechanics are introduced here.

## Form and evidence notes

### 31. Konan

Manga targets: 372–373 for paper techniques; 509–511 for the prepared confrontation. [VIZ chapter 510 locator](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/naruto-chapter-510/chapter/5220) identifies the primary review location; paid panels were not fully inspected. Rate the coherent final confrontation with its finite prepared explosive stock. The extraordinary trap raises attack (78), but setup and finite supplies lower stamina (22). It is not portable limitless ammunition in every random arena. Her ordinary paper movement/flight remains supported. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu’s composite band, below the ordinary Nagato regional ceiling. This model cannot perfectly encode a prepared location advantage.

### 32. Nagato

Manga targets: 548–551 for reanimation and absorbed-chakra restoration; 429–439 for earlier Pain techniques. [Official Naruto retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1611) locates the war confrontation; [VIZ chapter 550](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/naruto-chapter-550/chapter/5386) is a primary review entry. This is one restored reanimated body, not that body plus six separate Pain teammates. Chakra absorption is conditional and occurs before the selected snapshot; no infinite free refill is assumed. Mobility remains restrained despite restored appearance. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu and below Hashirama. **744 exceeds the preliminary 501–650 Nagato/Pain anchor band because that early band underestimated the selected restored Edo form.** Keep the actual formula result and document the mismatch; do not clamp it to fit the ladder. The earlier anchor is now explicitly provisional, pending the full audit.

### 33. Obito Uchiha

Manga targets: 638–653, particularly 640–645. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1706) documents stabilization of the Ten-Tails state and its later loss. Truth-Seeking Ball tools, barriers and the Sword of Nunoboko belong to the supported state. Exclude Kamui intangibility, the later dual-eye Kamui rescue state and independently controlled reanimated jinchuriki. Durability/recovery and large chakra reserves are strong, but sage-powered counters remain relevant. Relative anchors: above Hashirama’s 840 and below Madara’s final 923; the apparent gap is a compressed game index, not a physical multiplier.

### 34. Madara Uchiha

Manga targets: 673–678, plus 659–672 for earlier Ten-Tails context. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1663) locates his progress toward the final dream plan. Select the living final state with restored Rinnegan pair and forehead eye. Do not stack Edo immortality, Kakashi’s temporarily stolen eye, every previous weapon or an extra complete Kurama ally into that state. Infinite Tsukuyomi has activation/exposure conditions; Limbo is not automatically undetectable to every crossover opponent. Relative anchors: above Hashirama and the Six Paths protagonists’ existing composites, below Kaguya. Scores do not promise a solo victory in every matchup.

### 35. Kaguya Otsutsuki

Manga targets: 679–690. [Official Naruto retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1611) documents dimension shifts and the final team sealing. Choose the revived final-battle state. Her lethal bones, realm movement and enormous chakra are distinct advantages; unfamiliar tactics and sealing vulnerability remain weaknesses. The Expansive Truth-Seeking Ball depends on its demonstrated chakra/setup context, not a freely repeated instant attack. Low confidence reflects translating realm-scale effects into eight stats and the provisional planetary+ label. Relative anchors: above Hashirama and Madara; 953 is the Naruto roster ceiling, **not proof of measured planetary destruction or omnipotence**.

### 36. Kabuto Yakushi

Manga targets: 579–590. The [official Sage Mode character listing](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_2611) identifies the form; it is merchandise context rather than sufficient evidence for every technique. Detailed abilities require manga review. Incorporated traits were present in one supported Sage body, so sensory counters, recovery and terrain manipulation are not an incompatible composite. No named reanimated army is included in his individual score. White Rage has environmental/physiological effects, not a universal instant-win multiplier. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu and Jiraiya, below Hashirama and restored Nagato. Cave performance does not imply identical effectiveness on all terrain.

### 37. Killer B

Manga targets: 410–415 for sword/cloak techniques; 470–473, 566–572 for transformation and war context. [Official Naruto retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1611) locates the paired jinchuriki confrontation. Complete Eight-Tails partnership includes Gyuki as his canon transformation resource, not a second draft slot. Do not import Naruto’s separate chakra supply or later film battles. Swordplay and cloaks are retained skills, not seven independently controlled characters. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu and Kisame’s composite, below Hashirama. These values do not contradict Kisame’s favorable absorption matchup against chakra-rich hosts.

### 38. Fourth Raikage

Manga targets: 461–464 for the summit confrontation; 541–544 for the checkpoint battle and speed comparisons. Refer to the [official Naruto retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1611) for the checkpoint story context; panel review is still required for each named move. Select his strongest demonstrated living lightning-armor performance, not the Third Raikage’s body or Hell Stab. The earlier intact-arm state supports the full technique list without inventing a regenerated arm later. Speed 79 and durability 63 emphasize his profile; versatility 29 limits the scalar. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu and near Nagato’s original regional band. `imageQuery: "A"` is intentionally brief; Phase 3 must disambiguate by Naruto series and Fourth Raikage identity, rejecting ambiguous results.

### 39. Onoki

Manga targets: 464–466, 553–554, 560–563, 577–588. [Official Tsunade retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1868) locates the Five Kage battle. Particle Style, flight and weight manipulation belong to the elderly supported fighter; do not invent an unseen youthful prime or count Tsunade’s external chakra refill as his unlimited personal stamina. “Atomic Dismantling” is a recognizable translation, not a real-world atomic-energy measurement. High attack/intelligence are offset by frailty, mobility limits and finite reserves. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu’s composite, below Hashirama and the upper Nagato profile. A threatening technique need not place its user in the same tier as every possible victim.

### 40. Mei Terumi

Manga targets: 465–466 for the summit techniques; 563, 577–588 for war context. [Official Tsunade retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1868) provides team battle context, not proof of every individual attack. Lava, Boil and Water Release are supported; do not add ice, unrelated clan techniques or a claimed immunity to every corrosive attack. Skilled Mist depends on concentration, location and safety of nearby allies. Relative anchors: around Kakuzu’s general battlefield band, below Gaara’s stronger defensive composite and Nagato’s regional profile. Team Kage participation is not solo Madara scaling.
