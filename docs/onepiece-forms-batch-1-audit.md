## Coherent form inventories

- Luffy: retain all original eight snapshots. Add later pre-timeskip base, early Gear Two/Three, borrowed Nightmare and demonstrated early combined Gear burst. Nightmare requires supplied shadows, not permanent sword mastery or an extra army. Combined Gears occur after shadows depart, not all boosts stacked. No Gear One transformation or unsupported Tankman variant is invented.
- Zoro: early blades, Enies Lobby repertoire/first Asura, trained Shusui set, rooftop Enma/Asura and controlled King of Hell differ. A one-sword move can be a retained execution within a sword loadout; named style remains accurate. No stabilized advanced coating in the early burst. Controlled-style moves exclude an automatic simultaneous rooftop Asura bonus or unlimited medicine refill.
- Nami: mundane staff, original weather staff, Dial-enhanced Perfect Clima-Tact, Weatheria Sorcery tool and Zeus-housed tool differ. Zeus is a weapon partner, not Big Mom's whole homie arsenal. Weather preparation, cooperation and human durability remain.
- Usopp: early slingshot, Sogeking/Dial Kabuto, cultivated Black Kabuto and observed Haki-awakened era differ. Sogeking is an alias under the same identity; Haki awakening is not constant future sight. Ammunition and plant growth are finite.
- Sanji: early kicks, first Diable, trained Haki/Sky Walk, Raid Suit, awakening with ordinary Diable, and Ifrit differ. No suit invisibility in the destroyed-suit Ifrit state; contextual speed concealment is distinct. Social combat constraints, finite resources and imperfect body recovery remain.
- Chopper: thirteen snapshots include early Brain/Walk/Heavy/Jumping/Arm/Guard/Horn, uncontrolled Monster, trained Brain/Kung Fu/Horn, controlled three-minute Monster and extended thirty-minute Monster. Each locks the current body and excludes simultaneous defenses from other Points. Extra doses can destabilize early control. Extended duration requires prepared Caesar-assisted medicine with baby-like recovery. No confirmed awakening is inferred. Post-training Guard/Heavy/Walk refinements and aliases still need inventory review; thirteen does not mean certified exhaustive coverage.
- Robin: early blooms, trained giant limbs/clones, Gigante Fleur and Demonio differ. Bloom damage is shared and exposed giant parts have costs; dark shading does not prove Haki. Demonio uses a narrowed giant-body grappling list rather than stacking all earlier configurations.
- Franky: original cola cyborg, trained body laser and piloted General suit differ. General Cannon and body Radical Beam need distinct positions. General does not silently fire a pilot beam from inside the armor; exiting/repositioning is outside this locked-mecha move list. Equipment is finite and is not a teammate.
- Brook: early skeleton fencing and trained soul/cold sword era differ. Once-only revival is already spent; soul separation exposes the body. No confirmed Haki or universal soul destruction.
- Jinbe: demonstrated Marineford veteran and later Haki/karate toolkit differ. Water supply depends on arena; no separate transformation, unlimited ocean or guaranteed internal-water knockout.
- Ace/Shanks: explicit supported adult state rather than invented awakening/two-arm prime. Ace excludes novels and unrelated adaptations; Shanks excludes film moves and has low confidence due to sparse prolonged combat.
- Sabo: adult Dragon Claw before the inherited fire fruit and supported post-fruit toolkit differ. No child-prime estimate, awakening or advanced Conqueror coating inferred.
- Whitebeard: ill Marineford and shown Roger-era adulthood differ; terminal injuries are not combined with healthy endurance. Roger-era retained quake repertoire and long-fight stamina remain low-confidence inferences requiring review.
- Teach: darkness-only Banaro, newly acquired dual-fruit Marineford and later Emperor toolkit differ. Darkness increases pain and does not provide ordinary Logia intangibility. Contact fruit suppression does not automatically cancel chakra/reiatsu; no third fruit, awakening or invented acquisition explanation.
