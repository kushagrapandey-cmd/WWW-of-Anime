# 🍥 Naruto — Batch 1 audit (characters 1–15)

Approved rubric: [Phase 2](phase-2-power-rubric.md). Data: [`naruto.json`](../src/data/characters/naruto.json). Snapshot: Naruto chapters 1–700. Ratings are editorial estimates; each `confidence` covers form selection and scaling uncertainty, not mathematical correctness.

## Scope and evidence quality

Official Naruto retrospective columns were reviewed for form/event context and embedded chapter references. VIZ links locate supporting chapters, but their paid panels were not fully available for direct review here. Chapter ranges below are review targets, not a claim that every panel was inspected. Sparse feats, temporary borrowed resources and uncertain comparisons are noted explicitly. No film, Boruto, anime-only technique or hypothetical unseen adult upgrade is included.

Where translating named moves varies, use a recognizable English name. `Bashosen: Elemental Fan Attack` and `Summoning Technique: Ninja Tools` are descriptive labels for equipment use, not claims about a unique official English technique title. Phase 3 will verify `imageQuery` against API names and aliases.

## Per-character notes

### 1. Naruto Uzumaki

Manga targets: 671–675, 683–690, 696–698. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1611) supports Six Paths empowerment and the team victory over Kaguya. Peak is the final-duel chakra-avatar state, not Baryon Mode. Nature energy gathered through his own clone/Kurama coordination is a finite peak resource. Do not stack earlier expended Truth-Seeking Balls into that later snapshot; they are absent from this record’s moves/tags. Relative anchors: above Nagato’s regional tier and Hashirama’s strategic tier in this game model. The 912 score is not a claim of measured planetary destruction.

### 2. Sasuke Uchiha

Manga targets: 674–675, 692–698. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1858) supports the late-war Rinnegan and final conflict; detailed final-duel resources need panel review. Peak includes chakra taken from the captured tailed beasts, a finite canon resource allowed by the approved temporary-form rule. Exclude the earlier curse mark and later sequel upgrades. Relative anchors: above Nagato and Hashirama; close to Naruto by the shared model. Stamina 80 reflects finite resources; higher attack is offset by lower endurance/durability.

### 3. Sakura Haruno

Manga targets: 632–633, 676, 685, 689. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1743) documents regeneration, slug summoning and assistance against Kaguya. Stored chakra boosts recovery and striking, not permanent invulnerability. The team-assisted final punch is not solo Kaguya scaling. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu’s baseline composite band and around the Nagato calibration band; different strengths make this a game estimate, not a declared solo victory over either.

### 4. Kakashi Hatake

Manga targets: 688–691. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1904) documents Obito’s final gift, Perfect Susanoo and Kamui-assisted team tactics. Temporary chakra and both eyes belong to one supported state. Defense 95 rewards strong protection; durability 62 avoids treating his body as equally tough. Stamina 32 reflects the short-lived gift. Relative anchors: above Nagato and within the Hashirama strategic band, with a different matchup profile. No automatic Kamui win is assumed.

### 5. Might Guy

Manga targets: 668–672. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1673) describes the fatal final gate and its use against Madara. Attack/speed 95 represent the exceptional burst; stamina 8 and versatility 28 represent severe limits. Body destruction is not regeneration. Relative anchors: composite above Nagato’s regional band and within Hashirama’s broad strategic band. The index measures overall fighting capability; an Epic label does not deny the peak kick’s exceptional power. This exposes a genuine compromise in the approved eight-stat formula.

### 6. Rock Lee

Manga targets: 82–86 for established Lotus techniques; 617 and 669–672 for war context and gate use. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1652) confirms the taijutsu focus and earlier recovery, but does not fully document the Sixth Gate sequence. Exclude film-specific later feats and gifted chakra boosts supplied by other roster characters. Relative anchors: above Iruka’s local band, below the Nagato regional anchor; restricted versatility and gate strain keep the composite near the lower battlefield band.

### 7. Neji Hyuga

Manga targets: 100–104, 193–198, 256–258, 614. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1817) supports the clan techniques, growth and war sacrifice. Rate the later supported skill set, not a fictional surviving adult. Rotation is active defense (48); bodily durability remains 32. Relative anchors: above Iruka’s local band and around Kakuzu’s broad battlefield band. Precision and strategy do not imply Kakuzu-equivalent destructive output.

### 8. Hinata Hyuga

Manga targets: 437, 614–615, 629, 633. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1668) documents her progression into the war. Exclude Hamura chakra from The Last and unsupported adult improvements. External chakra gifts are not silently included in her base record. Relative anchors: above Iruka’s local band, below the center of Kakuzu’s battlefield band. Her techniques can disrupt opponents without granting automatic immunity-bypassing wins across series.

### 9. Shikamaru Nara

Manga targets: 325, 332–339, 529–534, 616, 633. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1910) supports the shadow techniques and planned defeat of Hidan. Intelligence 94 reflects demonstrated combat planning, not raw academic IQ alone. Preparation does not become extra attack points, and prebuilt lethal terrain is not always available in a random duel. Relative anchors: above Iruka’s local composite, below Kakuzu’s offensive profile. Common describes the score, not the usefulness of his tactics.

### 10. Choji Akimichi

Manga targets: 533–534, 633. The [official Shikamaru retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1910) documents the coordinated Asuma battle; detailed Butterfly Mode mechanics remain a primary-panel review item. Use the war breakthrough without mandatory pills. Do not treat his group’s success as a solo win against every participant. Relative anchors: above Iruka and in the lower Kakuzu battlefield band; strength/durability exceed speed and active protection.

### 11. Ino Yamanaka

Manga targets: 534, 611, 616, 633. The [official Team Ten retrospective context](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1910) supports the coordinated war role. Specific communications and mind-switch mechanics need direct chapter review. Avoid treating a brief assisted possession as domination of a much stronger target in a solo fight. Relative anchors: above Iruka’s local composite but below Kakuzu’s general combat band. Support value is larger than the damage-driven scalar captures.

### 12. Kiba Inuzuka

Manga targets: 75–77, 203–204, 633. [Chapter 633 locator](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/naruto-chapter-633/chapter/5474) is a direct source location; panels were not fully inspected in this environment. Akamaru is included as the inseparable canon fighting partner, not a separate roster pick. The three-headed transformation uses Kiba’s own clone and companion; it does not import another named character. Relative anchors: above Iruka, below Kakuzu; charging output and tracking have less utility against prepared defenses.

### 13. Shino Aburame

Manga targets: 69–70, 125–126, 633. [Chapter 633 locator](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/naruto-chapter-633/chapter/5474) anchors the war review. Insect cloning, tracking and chakra consumption are supported themes; full upper limits are uncertain. Exclude later sequel insects or another clan member’s abilities. Relative anchors: above Iruka, below Kakuzu. Intelligence/versatility support attrition without inventing large destructive output or assuming infinite bugs.

### 14. Tenten

Manga targets: 529 and 536–537 for equipment use/drain, plus established weapons summoning. [Chapter 529 locator](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/naruto-chapter-529/chapter/5365) identifies the equipment context. Briefly wielded Bashosen is allowed under the temporary canon equipment rule; the full Sage tool collection is not simultaneously mastered. Its broad elemental capability does not mean every possible element was independently demonstrated by her. Relative anchors: just above Iruka’s local band and below Kakuzu. Low confidence marks sparse independent feats and equipment uncertainty. No anime-only Twin Rising Dragons move is used.

### 15. Gaara

Manga targets: 248–249, 546–548, 560–563, 577–578. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1688) supports his continued sand protection after Shukaku’s extraction and the war-era leadership/father confrontation. Peak uses that later sand-control state, not a composite with childhood full-Shukaku transformation. Defense 76 is distinct from body durability 43. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu’s composite band and within Nagato’s regional band, below Hashirama. Large barriers and sealing raise utility without assuming unrestricted terrain or an invincible automatic shield.
