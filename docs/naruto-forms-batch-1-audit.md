## Coherence and evidence review

- Sasuke: first curse release precedes Chidori; Part One level two excludes Kirin and Mangekyo. Hebi has separate sword/curse profiles. Kirin needs storm setup. Summit Susanoo stages share one summit-era profile pending finer panel inventory. EMS excludes external curse donations. Rinnegan war form excludes captured-beast stock; Indra Susanoo includes that finite prepared resource.
- Sakura: Part One academy toolkit excludes medical/strength training. Medic and pre-seal war base exclude active regeneration. The released stored-chakra seal is finite; Katsuyu is not a free allied network.
- Kakashi: young Chidori has tunnel-vision risk; single Sharingan excludes dual-eye phasing/Susanoo. War Kamui excludes Kurama refills. Temporary dual eyes copy the selected 812 peak. Postwar base is low confidence, without novel-only Purple Lightning or an invented Hokage boost; ordinary Earth Wall evidence comes from an earlier retained technique.
- Guy: base, Sixth, Seventh and Eighth Gates have separate costs. Lower numbered gates are supported prerequisites but do not have independently demonstrated Guy combat profiles in this batch; inventory review must decide whether separate low-confidence transition entries are useful. No Lee-to-Guy move import. Eighth Gate keeps peak 688 with fatal duration cost.
- Lee: weighted/unweighted base, Gates 1–6, intoxicated fight and trained war base are distinct. Second Gate is a brief recovery step, not regeneration; Gates 3/4 also have low-confidence transitional ratings. No Seventh/Eighth Gate or Guy-exclusive gate attacks. The war Sixth Gate excludes donated Kurama cloak; peak 420 is retained, not scaled from external chakra.
- Neji/Hinata: earlier profiles do not inherit war moves. Blind spots, contact/range and finite chakra remain. No anime-only protective palms or The Last upgrades.
- Shikamaru: early and revenge-era profiles exclude war communications and free pre-installed terrain traps. Chakra-blade traps need equipment; Hidan’s prepared defeat is not an unconditional duel move.
- Choji: three separate medicine stages precede pill-free war butterfly control. Pill toxicity and calorie conversion limit stamina; no free medical rescue or food refills. Size changes used as attacks do not automatically become extra identity slots.
- Ino: early transfer excludes later sensory/network feats and leaves her body vulnerable. War communication does not import allies’ damage or the Kurama cloak.
- Kiba: Four Legs, Akamaru’s human double, two-headed fusion, trained base and three-headed wolf are distinct. Akamaru is the canonical partner, not a second draft slot. Three-headed fusion requires a shadow clone; signature moves in that snapshot are restricted to its body.
- Shino: early/war colony loadouts remain finite; drain is conditional. Giant cultivated insects and any materially different loadouts need the final inventory check; no limitless insect summons.
- Tenten: sparse ordinary-tool eras are low confidence. Bashosen is borrowed equipment with extreme chakra drain; no anime-only Twin Rising Dragons or combined Sage treasure set.
- Gaara: Part One base, partial and sleep-controlled full Shukaku, living Kazekage host and post-extraction war sand are separate. Waking the sleeping host interrupts beast control. Post-extraction sand remains, but no simultaneous beast or Edo regeneration.

## Calibration and remaining work

New terminal peak snapshots preserve existing identity stats for all 14 characters. Kiba’s three-headed form narrows its move list without changing the identity record. Temporary resource estimates remain provisional, especially Sasuke’s borrowed Indra stock, Lee’s cloak-free Sixth Gate and Kakashi’s short dual-eye gift. Peak preservation is compatibility, not evidence of completed balance review.

Early academy/local forms occupy the Common band. Mature support profiles remain near the original local shinobi anchors. Guy’s short lethal burst and Kakashi’s short dual-eye window demonstrate why output and overall score need not rise identically. Sasuke’s Six Paths profiles approach the existing Naruto/Madara ceiling without generic percentage multipliers. These are editorial compressed ratings; the final peer audit must check each new stat vector and sparse transitions.

No thresholds changed to hit rarity quotas. Missing states and alias checks remain open: curse-stage/eye subforms, Guy’s lower gates, external war chakra cloaks, distinct Shino colonies, relevant intermediate era snapshots and peak-vs-ordinary scope. No roster character is certified fully covered yet. Next continue Naruto identities 16–30, then 31–40, followed by One Piece and Bleach forms before Phase 3.

## Validation

Integration checks compare JSON IDs with browser exports, ensure every form preserves identity and replaces stats/moves, reject cross-character forms, verify retained peak stats and render the guide. Browser/mobile/keyboard layout review remains manual when Chromium is unavailable.
