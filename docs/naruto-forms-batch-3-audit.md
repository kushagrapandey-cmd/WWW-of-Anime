## Form scope and inventory decisions

- Konan: ordinary paper techniques and the final prepared explosive field differ. The field is finite and location-dependent; its stock is not freely supplied in an ordinary arena.
- Nagato/Pain: crippled living controller, six prepared corpses, remaining Deva body, depleted Edo and restored Edo are separate. The six-body snapshot stays one identity; no additional living/restored Nagato is added. The Deva-only snapshot excludes other Paths' moves. Body preparation, control range, cooldowns, destruction and sealing require explicit treatment in later gameplay. Rinne Rebirth is life-costly, not a repeatable heal.
- Obito: young Sharingan, Spiral Zetsu-assisted first Mangekyo burst, Konoha attack, spare-eye Izanagi loadout, war Rinnegan, unstable/stabilized Ten-Tails, depleted post-extraction rescue and dual-eye portal rescue are separate. No Kamui intangibility is assigned to Ten-Tails forms. Dual-eye Obito does not inherit Kakashi's later Susanoo. Sakura's supplied chakra is contextual, not a permanent refill. Post-extraction transitional eye/beast/Black Zetsu resources still need panel review.
- Madara: historical EMS, borrowed Kurama/Susanoo, modified Edo, revived blind Sage, one-Rinnegan Sage, successive Ten-Tails/Tree/eye states and final two-Rinnegan/forehead-eye state differ. A temporarily stolen Kakashi eye is never stacked with the final eye pair. Controlled Kurama requires preparation and is one form resource, not a second draft slot. Edo recovery does not carry into the living host.
- Kaguya: unstable beast mass is separate from precise human-form techniques. Contact absorption and descriptive lunges do not inherit the human body's tactical intellect or complete move list. The expansive ball retains its external chakra/setup cost; its existing peak stamina remains a calibration question.
- Kabuto: medical spy, pre-Sage graft assimilation and Snake Sage body differ. Sparse graft combat receives low confidence; no independent reanimated army is added to any personal score.
- Killer B: ordinary swords, Version One/Two cloaks, partial appendages, borrowed Samehada base and complete Gyuki differ. Full beast moves use ball/ink/tentacles rather than silently retaining a humanoid seven-sword stance. Samehada cooperation and external chakra are conditional; no Kisame fusion is inferred.
- Fourth Raikage: intact-arm armor and the later one-arm state remain separate. No invented numbered armor forms, Third Raikage Hell Stab or regenerated arm. Cooperative Double Lariat is excluded from solo moves.
- Onoki and Mei: one supported adult combat snapshot each; no distinct transformation or unseen youthful prime is invented. External Kage support is not personal infinite stamina; mist conditions and ally safety remain.

## Calibration and peak compatibility

Every new identity has a selected-peak form preserving its original eight stats, score and tier. Narrower body-specific move lists are deliberate: full Gyuki does not silently wield seven humanoid swords. The selected peak is last in each list, preserving the guide's existing default selection. Compatibility does not prove that the originally selected state has the highest score of every possible future snapshot.

All new vectors are individually authored; no generic multiplier, popularity bonus or rarity quota. Ordinary medical/sword/paper users remain near existing local/regional peers. Restored Nagato 744 is above six-body Pain 609; stable Obito 864 exceeds his transient uncontrolled 703. Madara's supported eye/host changes approach 923; his borrowed historical Kurama 793 does not bring final Rinnegan feats. Unstable Kaguya 700 represents sharply impaired control and finite evidence, not proof that her raw destructive output is below every Epic. These are editorial eight-stat comparisons, not literal physical ratios or guaranteed matchup outcomes.

Complete panel/alias inventories, omitted intermediate bases/loadouts, borrowed resources, prepared-state eligibility and peer calibration remain open for all series. In particular, Naruto's final-avatar stamina, Kaguya's external-chakra peak and location-dependent Konan need review before Phase 2 sign-off. No thresholds or identity rarity distributions change in this batch.
