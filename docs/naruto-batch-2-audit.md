# 🍥 Naruto — Batch 2 audit (characters 16–30)

Approved rubric: [Phase 2](phase-2-power-rubric.md). Dataset: [`naruto.json`](../src/data/characters/naruto.json). [Batch 1](naruto-batch-1-audit.md) remains semantically unchanged. This batch adds exactly 15 records; no new UI or battle behavior is implemented.

## Form selection and evidence limits

The Naruto 1–700 cutoff remains fixed. Official retrospectives were reviewed where available; manga ranges identify primary review targets. Paid chapter panels were not fully accessible, so these notes do not claim an exhaustive panel audit. Numerical scores and cross-series translation are editorial estimates, not canon facts.

For the reanimated Hokage and Itachi, the recorded form includes the supported reanimation state. Regeneration improves recovery/endurance; it does not make the body impossible to damage or grant an automatic win against sealing, restraint or other supported counters. Finite 1–100 ratings compress this capability for the approved game. No additional engine mechanics are invented in this batch.

Living complete arsenals are selected for Hashirama, Sasori, Deidara and Kakuzu where those sequences provide the clearest supported combat profile. We do not combine their living equipment/feats with assumed reanimation upgrades. Whether later reanimated states should replace those snapshots is explicitly a review item, particularly for Deidara/Kakuzu. “Peak” means the strongest supported coherent form, not an idealized loadout assembled from separate scenes.

Descriptive move labels identify the use of canon tools where English naming varies: Kusanagi Sword Attack, Triple-Bladed Scythe Assault and Puppet Body: Flamethrower are not claimed as unique official translation titles. Image queries use recognizable character names; API spelling and aliases are verified in Phase 3.

## Per-character audit

### 16. Temari

Manga targets: 213–214 for weasel summoning; 553–554 for war-era wind attacks. [Official Shikamaru retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1910) supports her earlier fan-based matchup; it is not evidence for every later upgrade. Do not import Boruto feats. Fan range and broad wind pressure improve attack/versatility; the summoned weasel is a normal technique resource, not an independent roster character. Relative anchors: above Iruka’s local band, toward the lower Kakuzu battlefield band. No guaranteed penetration of superior armor is assumed.

### 17. Kankuro

Manga targets: 250–251, 518–519. [Official Sasori retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1641) describes their earlier duel and Kankuro’s later use of Sasori’s former puppet body. Owning that shell does not transfer Sasori’s living chakra core, complete human-puppet collection or personal Iron Sand capability. Older Crow/Black Ant trap techniques remain learned equipment skills, not proof he operates every puppet at once. Relative anchors: above Iruka, below Kakuzu’s composite. Human durability is separated from puppet defenses.

### 18. Jiraiya

Manga targets: 372–383. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1643) documents Sage Mode and the Pain confrontation. Imperfect Sage Mode includes demonstrated elder-toad cooperation for nature energy and combined techniques; those summoned partners are not extra draft characters. Goemon is cooperative, not a solo one-step attack. Preparation and limited knowledge of Pain remain constraints. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu’s band, within Nagato’s regional band. The same score as Tsunade reflects different stat profiles, not identical abilities.

### 19. Tsunade

Manga targets: 169–171, 577–578, 591. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1868) documents regeneration and the Five Kage confrontation. Use her stored-chakra state without adding Dan’s later emergency refill as an unlimited personal reserve. Durability/recovery 71 differs from active defense 47; repeated healing still costs chakra and does not guarantee immunity to dismemberment. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu and around Nagato’s regional band; below Hashirama’s strategic profile.

### 20. Orochimaru

Manga targets: 291–296 for body replacement/sword techniques; 593 for revival; 618–620 for restored arms and vessel change. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1629) supports the general body-transfer pursuit and loss/recovery story context. His exact full post-restoration combat ceiling is sparse, so confidence is low. Retained techniques are plausible; no hypothetical new Wood Release arsenal or unlimited Hashirama power is assigned. Reanimated named Hokage are independent combatants and excluded from his score. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu, near Nagato’s upper regional band, below Hashirama.

### 21. Hashirama Senju

Manga targets: 620–626 for the living rivalry/Valley of the End flashback; 631–638 for comparison context. [Official retrospective locator](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1627) was found, but full-page retrieval failed; it does not count as direct verification of every feat. Living Sage Mode supports enormous wood constructs and tailed-beast suppression. Do not add Edo recovery to that living body. His 840 rating sits inside his approved 651–850 strategic anchor band, below the final Six Paths protagonists. Relative anchors: above Nagato, serving as the Hashirama reference itself. This score is not a country-destruction measurement.

### 22. Tobirama Senju

Manga targets: 618–620, 638–642, 661. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1694) supports the Second Hokage’s history; detailed war tactics still require primary-panel review. War reanimation enables the demonstrated self-sacrificial explosive-tag sequence; it is not a free projectile with no setup. Flying Thunder God needs usable marks, and teleportation is not infinite continuous running speed. Relative anchors: above Nagato’s band and below Hashirama. Intelligence 92 and stamina 95 do not make his body unbreakable (durability 57).

### 23. Hiruzen Sarutobi

Manga targets: 117–123 for Enma and clones; 618–620 and 660–662 for war-era elemental combinations. [VIZ chapter 660 locator](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/naruto-chapter-660/chapter/5501) provides the primary review entry; its paid panels were not fully inspected. Rate observed older reanimation, not an unseen youthful “strongest Hokage” assertion. All-five-nature versatility differs from all-five-nature maximum power. Enma is a canon summon/tool, not another drafted hero. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu, around Nagato’s upper band and well below Hashirama.

### 24. Minato Namikaze

Manga targets: 630–631, 640–645. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1681) explicitly documents Kurama Link Mode after reanimation and Flying Thunder God teamwork. Select that coherent avatar state with intact arms; do not stack separate later Sage Mode moments into it. Teleport marks and finite attack throughput still matter. Stamina 94 compresses the regenerative resource advantage; sealing/disruption are not ignored. Relative anchors: above Nagato, near but below Hashirama’s full composite. Extremely high mobility need not imply equal bodily toughness.

### 25. Itachi Uchiha

Manga targets: 549–552, 578–590. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1814) documents his illness before death and reanimated return. Rate the free-willed war state; omit the crow’s already-used borrowed Kotoamatsukami as a freely reusable personal eye power. Susanoo/Totsuka tools are supported, but must connect and satisfy relevant conditions. Izanami is not an unconditional instant-win boost and is omitted from the short signature list. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu/Nagato bands, below Hashirama’s composite. Active defense 88 differs from bodily durability 52.

### 26. Kisame Hoshigaki

Manga targets: 470–473 for Samehada fusion and moving water prison; 506–508 for Guy confrontation. [Official Guy retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1673) documents the chakra-absorbing shark technique and its defeat by Guy’s taijutsu response. Do not confuse the real Kisame with the earlier reduced-chakra impersonation. Samehada is cooperative equipment for this snapshot; its loyalty and absorption conditions are not universal guarantees. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu and around Nagato’s regional/strategic boundary. No infinite refill from an opponent with incompatible powers is assumed.

### 27. Deidara

Manga targets: 248–249, 357–363; 518–519 for the separate reanimation comparison. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1781) documents different clay grades and the final self-detonation. The living complete arsenal is supported without assuming a repeatable Edo-C0 loop. Attack 76 captures burst scope; stamina 19 captures committing his life to C0. C4 has delivery/counter conditions, not guaranteed damage to every target. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu’s composite band but below Nagato’s regional profile. The chosen peak-state policy is reviewable if direct war evidence supports a better coherent form.

### 28. Sasori

Manga targets: 264–274; 518–519 for the separate reanimated encounter. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1641) documents Hiruko, Iron Sand, the hundred puppets and his vulnerable living core. Select the living retained arsenal; the war encounter does not show the same complete equipment state. Defeating a nation with a puppet force is not proof of destroying country-sized terrain in one strike. Poison is counterable and requires exposure. Relative anchors: above Kakuzu’s lower battlefield profile, below Nagato’s regional upper range. Shell damage can expose his core; no automatic immortality tag is used.

### 29. Hidan

Manga targets: 323–339. [Official retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1749) documents the ritual and his defeat. A victim’s blood and the ritual circle are necessary; self-inflicted injury outside that setup does not automatically transfer. Immortality is survival, not fast self-regeneration or immunity to decapitation/restraint. Duration tolerance improves stamina, not active defense. Relative anchors: above Iruka’s local composite, below Kakuzu’s general profile. Common is the computed tier despite a dangerous conditional ability.

### 30. Kakuzu

Manga targets: 334–342; 530–534 as the separate war-state review. [Official Kakashi retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1903) and [Shikamaru retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1910) locate the immortal-duo battle; every mask interaction still requires manga review. Choose the demonstrated living complete five-heart configuration. A reanimated full-heart configuration is not silently substituted; review its demonstrated loadout before revising the peak. Earth hardening has a documented lightning counter. Multiple hearts add finite recovery options, not infinite replacement without victims. Relative anchors: Kakuzu establishes the approved 301–500 band; below Nagato. The computed anchor score is 481.
